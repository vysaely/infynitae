# C12.F19 — prepared amendment package (Phase F: layer surfaces) — PREPARED, NOT ADOPTED

Status: **PREPARED.** Records the normative consequences of the owner decisions L1–L8
(`DECISION_RECORD_2026-10-21.md`). Until adopted (layer text, `F_LAYERS`, INV rows, fixtures)
the living registries are unchanged and this package is not normative.

- **OBJ-35 LayerSurface (L1):** owner Core `layer`; writers the protocol entries
  `layer_created`/`layer_committed`/`layer_destroyed` and output removal; readers render, hit
  testing, focus, placement, fit; lifetime: the surface or its output; forbidden writers: cameras,
  Stage. Not an element: INV-STAGE-*, INV-CAMERA-* and membership do not apply to it.
- **F-HIT-1 extension (L3):** the per-output hit order overlay → top → ProjectionFrame → bottom →
  background; a layer target's pointer frame is surface-local (origin 0).
- **F-PLACE-1 and F-FIT-1 (L4):** "usable region" is the output view less the exclusive zones of
  its layer surfaces (arranged per the protocol, in creation order); placement centres in it;
  fit fits into it and shows the window's centre at its centre (`fit_target_in`). Unchanged when
  nothing is reserved.
- **M12 / INV-INPUT-8 (L5):** a layer surface may hold the keyboard (exclusive or on demand); the
  ActiveElementFocus continues underneath and receives the keyboard when the layer lets go; an
  exclusive holder is not displaced by element focus changes.
- **C5 presentation (L6):** only `overlay` layer surfaces are shown on an output that presents.
- **INV-OUTPUT-1:** an output's layer surfaces leave and are closed before its global goes.
