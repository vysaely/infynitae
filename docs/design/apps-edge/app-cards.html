App cards: a first look

The cards of stage 2 in version 1 of the system cards (a header with the title and the app the card comes from, rows, the light under the pointer, buttons that appear on hover). A real panel is one column; here the cards stand in columns to fit a page. Under each card: its word in a PANEL_ line and what a click does.

What is real here: the time and the month, the Steam games and their order (the owner's library on 2026-11-13), the drives I to IV and their sizes, the installed apps in "Add a card". What is an example: the monitor's numbers, the song, the Discord messages, the Firefox windows. The squares with a letter stand for the apps' own icons, and the plain square for the song's cover.

The Discord card shows what Discord sent as notifications and its windows. Discord gives no unread count, so none is drawn.
