Settings cards

The cards of docs/EDGE_SETTINGS.md in the version 1 look: a header with the title and "Settings" at the right, rows with a switch, a value or a slider, More for the rest, lists opening in place. Values are the defaults of config/config; the screen, fonts and the PC's parts are examples of what a PC reports. The page is a drawing: nothing on it changes a setting.

The Settings panel (Super+I)

One search field, one index card with a row per group, This PC. A click on a row opens that group in place; here Appearance is open.

Each card on its own, at its default size (S)

Any of them can be added to any panel. At M the rows under More are always shown.

Open question: a row with a few values

A: the value at the right, a click opens the values in place (40 px a row, the same for 2 values or 6). B: the values always shown as words under the name (about 70 px a row, one click less, long words do not fit).

Colours are the theme's: card #171013 at 90%, outline #452e3c, text #eef2f8, muted #a9aaaf, accent #cc70a9, tracks the text colour at 12%. Corners 8 px, small parts 4 px. A value that differs from its default is drawn in the accent; "At the next start" marks a change that waits for a restart. The small squares in Appearance show the setting's own value.
